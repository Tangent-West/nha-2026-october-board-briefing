Executive Summary

What the First Six Months Delivered

The April 21 leadership transition coincided with federal policy uncertainty, strained industry relationships, recovery of critical NHA accounts, and the HEMI and Goodness of Hemp® launch. Sully Sullivan and Laura Franklin, working through Tangent West alongside the Board, HEMI, and contributing partners, restored the operating foundation and used it to deliver national research, USDA applications, communications, advocacy, and partnerships.

When I stepped into the Executive Director role, our first responsibility was to regain access to the systems that let NHA serve members and operate day to day. Laura led much of the operational recovery and communications work. With the Board, HEMI, and our partners, we used that restored capacity to publish a national benchmark, submit two USDA applications, and renew direct engagement with the industry.

Research, Funding, and Advocacy

Published September 29

National Survey and Benchmark Report

Participants from at least 36 states contributed to NHA's inaugural national benchmark. Voluntary respondents rated industry conditions an average of 2.25 out of 5. The report gives NHA a reference point for policy, programming, communications, research, and market development.

Two FY2027 Applications Submitted

USDA and Funding Development

The Market Access Program (MAP) application focused on exporter readiness, buyer engagement, and international market development. The Emerging Markets Program (EMP) application proposed technical market-access work in Malawi, Rwanda, Zimbabwe, and Zambia. The two USDA/FAS applications were supported by 29 letters.

NHA also supported the Hawaii Fibers/Hemp Paper Company proposal and worked with Shawn Ledig and Delta AgroFiber Solutions on Louisiana research and funding collaboration.

Funding stages. Applications are submitted. Awards, funding commitments, and cash received each require separate verification.
