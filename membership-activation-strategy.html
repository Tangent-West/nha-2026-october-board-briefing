Membership Recovery and Next Steps

Recovered and Established

WishList Member records, levels, and access information were recovered along with historical payment visibility. In November, NHA established HubSpot, connected QuickBooks Online, and set up the membership invoicing workflow.

Verification Underway

Reconcile paid members against historical records and collected dues. Verify end-to-end invoice sync before treating the new workflow as a source for Board reporting.

Recruitment and Communications

Use survey findings in social posts, newsletters, and event conversations. Leadership assesses that social has historically contributed limited paid membership. Recruit through trusted introductions, partner events, and direct follow-up. Track qualified conversations, paid joins, renewals, and collected dues by source.

Website and Member Value

Schedule the full overhaul after the holidays. Improve the experience for joining and renewing, make research easier to find, and increase committee visibility.
