Campaign Delivery and Next Steps

During July through October, NHA and HEMI coordinated education, communications, and coalition partner materials. By November 10, the updated Goodness of Hemp website provided a place to share campaign education and direct readers to the policy materials.

For the next engagement, use national survey findings in social content and connect them to newsletters, direct conversations, and partner events. Define the audience, NHA's role, owner, expected cost, and intended outcome for each proposed event or sponsorship opportunity.

Education
