NHA 2026 October Board Briefing
Reporting through November 10, 2026

Executive Summary
The April 21 leadership transition coincided with federal policy uncertainty,
strained industry relationships, recovery of critical accounts, and the launch
of HEMI and the Goodness of Hemp campaign. Sully Sullivan and Laura Franklin,
through Tangent West, worked alongside the Board, HEMI, and contributing
partners to restore NHA operations and deliver national research, USDA
applications, communications, advocacy, and partnerships.

Original Plan and Delivery
The plan called for foundation work in days 0-90, activation in days 90-120,
execution through day 180, and measurement and expansion afterward.

April 21-July 20
- Audited 30+ backend systems, repaired payment and checkout functions, and
  restored initial financial visibility and historical member records.
- Rolled out leadership and the campaign, engaged the Board and SCOHO, and
  reactivated CongressPlus through The Soft Edge on June 24. Tell Congress followed.

July 21-October 20
- Conducted the national survey and published the inaugural benchmark on
  September 29, with participants from at least 36 states. Voluntary,
  self-reported ratings averaged 2.25 out of 5 for industry conditions.
- Submitted separate FY2027 USDA/FAS Market Access Program (MAP) and Emerging
  Markets Program (EMP) applications with 29 letters of support. MAP addressed
  exporter readiness, buyer engagement, and international market development.
  EMP proposed technical market-access work in Malawi, Rwanda, Zimbabwe, and Zambia.
- Resumed newsletters and dedicated emails, published leadership blogs,
  developed the LinkedIn newsletter, coordinated with HEMI, prepared policy
  and partner materials, and participated in research and industry events.
- CongressPlus recorded 3,389 cumulative activities as of October 14.
  A November total requires a current export.

October 21-November 10
- Participated in ABLC NEXT and advanced the updated Goodness of Hemp site.
- Established HubSpot, connected QuickBooks Online, and set up the membership
  invoicing workflow. Paid-member reconciliation and invoice sync verification
  remain open.
- Prepared refreshed SCOHO member bios and logos. Publication remains to be
  confirmed. SCOHO and SCIHO provided support letters.
- Planned for the onsite USDA/FAS compliance review.

System Recovery
PayPal merchant account for account access and transaction visibility. PayPal
Payflow Gateway for website payment processing and recurring billing. PayPal
Manager for Payflow administration. Intuit QuickBooks for accounting visibility.
Truist for banking and treasury access. BILL/Divvy for staff finance access.
WishList Member for historical records, levels, and access. WordPress and Divi
for functionality, checkout, publishing, and updates. SiteGround for hosting.
LastPass for credential management. Constant Contact for newsletters and
historical audience access. Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn Page
for administration and publishing. CongressPlus through The Soft Edge for
advocacy. SAM.gov, UEI, and USDA/FAS Unified Export Strategy for registration,
readiness, and administration. GoDaddy, Google Workspace, and the Executive
Director mailbox were included in the handoff. Confirm ownership and transfer.

Program Notes
- FY2024 EMP assessments in Malawi, Rwanda, and Ghana were completed. Verify
  financial reporting, reimbursement, and administrative closeout separately.
- NHA supported the Hawaii Fibers/Hemp Paper Company proposal and collaborated
  with Shawn Ledig and Delta AgroFiber Solutions on Louisiana research and funding.
- Record applications, awards, funding commitments, and cash received separately.
  Verify fundraising outcomes before reporting figures.
- HEMI leads Goodness of Hemp education and marketing. NHA leads and funds
  policy advocacy.

Events and Industry Relationships
- July 27, U.S. Biochar Coalition webinar. Laura and Sully presented Seed to
  Sequestration: The Industrial Hemp & Biochar Connection. Laura represented
  HITA. Sully represented NHA and HITA.
- September 10, USDA-ARS stakeholder event at Cornell AgriTech in Geneva,
  New York. Represented NHA and HITA virtually in a stakeholder presentation
  connected to the national hemp germplasm program.
- October 8, Hemp in the Built Environment, Belgrade, Montana. Supported
  IND HEMP with promotion, on-site filming support, and recap coverage.
- October 21-23, ABLC NEXT, San Francisco. Engaged partners across agriculture,
  biobased materials, and industrial supply chains.

Next Engagement and Board Decisions
- Schedule the NHA website overhaul after the holidays. Improve joining,
  renewals, research access, committee visibility, and member value.
- Use survey findings for social content, newsletters, conversations, and
  events. Leadership assesses that social media has contributed limited paid
  membership. Prioritize trusted introductions, partner events, and direct
  follow-up. Track qualified conversations, paid joins, renewals, and
  collected dues by source.
- Pursue grants, sponsorships, and event partnerships. Define the audience,
  NHA role, owner, expected cost, and intended outcome for each opportunity.
- Explore joint leadership of the existing Standing Committee for Social
  Equity with its current leaders. Identify a participation barrier and an
  initial project with the community it would serve.
- Submit the USDA/FAS participant profile and supporting records by November
  20, 2026. Prepare for the March 1-5, 2027 onsite review in Washington, D.C.
  Seek partner meetings, sponsorship conversations, and awareness events.
- Align the next contract's priorities, owners, budget, and measures. Request
  sponsor introductions, event partnerships, direct member outreach, and
  committee participation.
- Review monthly with management and report quarterly to the Board on paid
  membership, collected dues, funding stages, event follow-up, and compliance.
