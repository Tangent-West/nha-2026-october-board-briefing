The Goodness of Hemp Act

The Goodness of Hemp Act is a proposed federal policy framework. NHA leads and funds policy advocacy, and HEMI leads education and marketing for the Goodness of Hemp® Campaign.

Policy Framework

Responsible Policy for American Hemp.

The Act gives NHA a clear 2026 policy initiative around education, stakeholder engagement, coalition building, industry support, and responsible policy.

Education

Explain the policy framework in language that policymakers, members, farmers, and industry partners can use.

Stakeholder Engagement

Create a disciplined path for farmers, manufacturers, members, and supporters to participate.

Coalition Building

Align industry voices around shared priorities and responsible market development.

Industry Support

Give NHA a policy platform that supports member value, partner engagement, and public credibility.

Responsible Policy

Position industrial hemp as agriculture, manufacturing, materials innovation, and economic opportunity.

Market Confidence

Help create regulatory clarity that supports farmers, investors, buyers, and sponsors.

Campaign Awareness. Policy Leadership.
